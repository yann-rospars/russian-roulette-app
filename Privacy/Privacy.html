Privacy Policy

Russian Roulette – Party Game

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Russian Roulette – Party Game ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

However, the App uses third-party advertising services that may collect or process certain information from your device.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, interactions with advertisements, diagnostic information, and other information necessary to provide, measure, personalize, and protect advertising services.

The information processed depends on your device, region, applicable privacy laws, and the privacy choices you make.

You can learn more about how Google handles information here: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Consent and European Users

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App may ask for your consent before using certain advertising technologies.

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent and privacy choices.

Depending on your choices, advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise limited as required by applicable law.

4. iOS Privacy and Tracking

On iOS, Apple may require the App to request permission before allowing tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies. You can change tracking permissions at any time in your iOS settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The App currently uses Google AdMob and related Google Mobile Ads services for advertising and consent management.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that personal information from a child has been collected improperly, please contact us.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and device, you may have rights or privacy choices regarding the processing of your information.

Where applicable, these choices can be managed through the consent interface provided in the App or through your device settings.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

The App has no user accounts, developer-operated backend, or user database. We do not maintain server-side player profiles or gameplay histories. Game progress is held in memory during use and is not stored in a user database. There is therefore no in-app account to delete.

Google UMP and advertising SDKs may store consent settings and other SDK data on your device. You can review or withdraw available advertising choices using "Privacy options" on the home screen when this option is required and available, and manage tracking permission in your device settings. Removing the App removes its local app data according to your operating system's rules; this does not automatically delete data already processed by Google or its partners, or copies in device backups.

Google and its advertising partners determine retention periods for data they process according to their policies, the purposes of processing and applicable obligations. We do not set a single retention period for those third-party data or have direct access to delete them. For Google's retention practices and available deletion controls, see Google's data retention policy and Google's Privacy Policy. Withdrawing consent affects subsequent processing where applicable; it does not itself erase data previously collected.

If you contact us by email, your message and email address are processed to respond to your request. Correspondence is retained only as necessary to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You may contact yga.rospars@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of correspondence we hold, or for help identifying the appropriate third-party privacy contact.
